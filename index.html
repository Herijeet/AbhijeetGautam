06 / About

I like the unglamorous part: making it fast and keeping it working.

I studied Information Technology in Kolkata and joined ICRA Analytics in 2023. Most of my work sits between a SQL database and an analyst's screen: APIs in C# and ASP.NET Core, front ends in Angular, and the data plumbing that keeps millions of rows moving every day.

  • Measure first

    Profile, find the real bottleneck, then fix that. The 2-hour job didn't need a bigger server.

  • Make it self-serve

    If people keep asking for the same kind of change, give them a tool to make it themselves.

  • Secure by default

    Auth, rate limits and headers go in early, not after the audit report arrives.
